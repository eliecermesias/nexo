# Nexo Requirements

## Functional Scope

### User Management

Users must manage personal, professional, tax, business, banking, document, template, sequence, and security preferences.

### Client Companies

Each `ClientCompany` must store tax identification, contact information, billing information, payment conditions, compliance configuration, required documents, delivery rules, PDF package rules, and active status.

### Services Catalog

Users must manage `Service` records with name, description, category, pricing type, base price, currency, tax rules, and active status.

### Service Valuation

`ServiceValuationService` must calculate subtotals, taxes, discounts, retentions, totals, paid amounts, and pending balances for quotes, proposals, invoices, payment claims, and final charges.

### Quotes

`Quote` must include client company, quote number, service items, prices, discounts, taxes, retentions, subtotal, total amount, currency, validity date, status, template version, terms, and notes.

### Proposals

`Proposal` must include scope, objectives, services, deliverables, timeline, commercial conditions, financial values, currency, template, version, and status.

### Invoices

The system must support internal support documents and external legal invoices. External invoice numbers must be unique for the user and client company.

### Payment Claims

`PaymentClaim` must include client company, document number, charged services, total amount, currency, bank account, payment method, required documents, compliance result, PDF package, status, issue date, due date, and notes.

### Compliance Matrix

Each `ClientCompany` can have a `ComplianceMatrix` defining required documents for a `PaymentClaim`.

Requirement fields include:

- Document type.
- Requirement name.
- Mandatory or optional.
- Requires expiration date.
- Requires approval.
- Requires file upload.
- Can be generated by system.
- Must be attached separately.
- Must be merged into final PDF.
- Merge order.
- Renewal frequency.
- Blocking rule.
- Validation rule.
- Active status.

### Compliance Validation Engine

`ComplianceValidationService` must return global status, valid documents, missing documents, expired documents, rejected documents, pending approval documents, blocking reasons, and suggested actions.

### PDF Packages

The system must generate PDFs from templates, merge uploaded and generated documents, preserve merge order, add cover pages or tables of contents when configured, store files securely, and retain generation history.

## Business Rules

1. Users cannot access another user's data.
2. Every business document belongs to an authenticated owner scope.
3. A `PaymentClaim` cannot be submitted when mandatory blocking requirements are missing.
4. Uploaded documents must be linked to the user, client company, document requirement, and business document.
5. Expired required documents invalidate compliance unless the requirement is optional or non-blocking.
6. External invoice numbers must not be duplicated for the same user and client company.
7. Generated PDF packages must retain version, hash, generation user, and generation timestamp.
8. Internal sequences must prevent duplicates and keep history.
9. Client compliance rules must be configurable, not hard-coded.
10. File downloads require authorization checks.
