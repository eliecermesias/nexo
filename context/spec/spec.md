# Nexo Product Specification

## Executive Summary

Nexo is a modular SaaS web application for managing the full lifecycle of business documents: quotes, proposals, invoices, payment claims, required documents, compliance validation, PDF generation, PDF merging, sequences, banking data, currencies, payment methods, templates, traceability, and documentary quality controls.

The system is intended for freelancers, consultants, auditors, teachers, instructors, small businesses, and service providers that must invoice or collect from client companies with different documentary requirements.

## Product Vision

Nexo helps users prepare, validate, package, and submit commercial and collection documents with evidence, traceability, and configurable compliance rules per client company.

## Target Users

- Independent professionals.
- Consultants and auditors.
- Teachers and instructors.
- Small service companies.
- Providers that submit payment claims to companies.

## Core Capabilities

- User Management.
- Client Companies.
- Services Catalog.
- Service Valuation.
- Quotes.
- Proposals.
- Invoices.
- Payment Claims.
- Compliance Matrix.
- Required Documents.
- Uploaded Documents.
- PDF Packages.
- Editable Templates.
- Finance.
- Sequence Management.
- Audit Trail.
- Security.

## Product Principles

- Each user operates in an isolated data scope.
- Client companies can define their own compliance rules.
- Every generated or uploaded document must be traceable.
- Business documents must be versionable when their relevant content changes.
- Security, authorization, and ownership validation are mandatory in every workflow.
- Tests drive implementation.
